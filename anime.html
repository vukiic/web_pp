Anime & Manga

Discover the art, storytelling, and creativity behind Japan’s most iconic modern export.

Stories that travel worldwide

Anime and manga are more than entertainment — they are a major part of Japanese culture and a global creative movement. Through unique visual style, emotional storytelling, and unforgettable characters, anime has shaped modern media across the world.

Manga, as a powerful storytelling format, influences everything from animation and cinema to fashion, games, and fan communities. Together, anime and manga represent Japan’s modern imagination.

Anime culture

Anime covers every genre imaginable — from action and fantasy to romance, mystery and sci-fi. It is known for expressive animation, cinematic music, and strong emotional moments that connect deeply with audiences.

Many anime series also reflect Japanese society, values, and everyday life, blending real-world themes with creative storytelling.

Popular genres

  • Shōnen (action & adventure)
  • Slice of life
  • Fantasy & isekai
  • Psychological / thriller

Manga world

Manga is Japan’s most influential form of visual storytelling. It ranges from short stories to epic series spanning decades. Manga magazines and volumes are a daily part of reading culture in Japan.

Many legendary anime series started as manga first, allowing creators to develop strong plots, character arcs, and unique artistic style.

Why manga is special

  • Strong focus on storytelling
  • Unique art and panel composition
  • Massive variety of themes
  • Global fan communities

Famous studios

Behind the visuals and storytelling are animation studios that bring manga panels and scripts to life. Many studios became legendary for their signature style, color palettes, and emotional atmosphere.

Studios also influence trends in animation techniques and contribute to Japan’s creative industry worldwide.

Known for style & impact

  • Studio Ghibli
  • MAPPA
  • Kyoto Animation
  • Toei Animation
